Foldout

The news, as a paper that ends.

Every morning Foldout folds the day's news into a short paper you read from the front page to the last, and then you're done. No endless feed, no alert for every story.

Get it on the App Store

A paper that ends

  • A front page with the biggest stories, then a page for each section you follow.
  • When several newsrooms cover the same story, Foldout prints it once, with a link to each.
  • A finish line tells you when you've read it all, and the day's papers wait on the breakfast table.

Your paper

  • Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, British and American papers, with local pages and the weather where you live, and an International edition.
  • The day page: the electricity price, currencies and on this day.
  • In English, Danish, Norwegian and Swedish.

Free, and Foldout Plus

The morning paper and the International morning edition are free. Foldout Plus adds the lunch and evening editions, the Weekend Foldout, a month of back issues, papers from other countries, and takes the sponsor boxes away.
