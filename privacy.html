Privacy policy

Foldout for iPhone · Last updated 6 November 2026

Foldout is a calm newspaper: each morning it folds the day's news into a short paper you read to the end. We collect as little as we can, never sell anything about you, and don't track you across other apps or websites.

No account, no name, no email

You don't sign in to use Foldout. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.

What the app sends to Foldout's server

  • How the paper is read. Anonymous events such as "the paper was opened", "a page was turned", "a story was opened", "the paper was finished" and how long that took. They carry a random number the app makes when it's installed, so we can tell one reader's reading from another's without knowing who anyone is, and the kind of iPhone (for example "iPhone17,1"). We use them only to make the paper better: which sections are read, where people stop, what's too long.
  • Your feedback, if you choose to write some at the end of the paper.
  • Errors, such as a paper that couldn't be loaded, so we can fix them.
  • Your place for the weather, only if you choose one. If you use "where I am", the app asks iPhone for your location once and sends only its coordinates rounded to about a kilometre, to fetch the forecast. Your exact location never leaves the phone, and nothing is sent in the background.

Your settings, saved stories and the papers themselves stay on your iPhone.

Purchases

Foldout Plus is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or Apple Account details. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account has Plus.

Links to newspapers

When you open a story at its newspaper, the newspaper's page opens in Safari inside the app. That newspaper's own privacy policy and cookies apply there, not ours. Photos in the paper are loaded from Foldout's server.

What we don't do

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics companies: the reading events go only to Foldout's own server.
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone.

How long we keep it

Reading events, feedback and errors are kept for at most 13 months, then deleted. Places sent for the weather aren't stored.

Where it's kept

Foldout's server runs on Cloudflare, which stores and serves the papers and the reading events for us.

To make the forecast for your own place, the server passes its rounded coordinates to MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute), and a town you search for to Photon (komoot's search of OpenStreetMap). Neither gets anything that says who you are.

Your rights

Because nothing we keep is tied to your name, we can't look you up. If you'd like your reading events deleted, tell us the random number from Settings → About in the app, and we'll delete everything recorded with it. Deleting the app also deletes that number, so a new install starts afresh. In the EU and EEA you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Foldout is a general news app and isn't made for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect anything from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be here with a new date.
