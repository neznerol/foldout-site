Foldout support

Questions, a problem, or an idea for the paper? Write to dennislundlorenzen@gmail.com. We read everything and usually answer within a couple of days.

When does the paper come?

The morning paper is printed at about five in the morning, Danish time. With Foldout Plus, a lunch edition comes at noon and an evening edition at six, and the Weekend Foldout on Saturday morning.

Restoring Foldout Plus on a new iPhone

Open the app, go to Foldout Plus and tap Restore purchases. Plus belongs to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any iPhone signed in with it.

Cancelling a subscription

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, choose Foldout and tap Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds: ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
